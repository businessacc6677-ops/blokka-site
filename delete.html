Delete my data

Blokka is published by G-Games. You can delete the data we store about you at any time, free of charge.

Option 1: in the app

Open Blokka, go to Settings, and tap Delete my online data. This removes your online profile, name, scores and duel record from our servers immediately.

Option 2: by email (if you uninstalled the app)

Email mortadamortada313m@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the name you used in the game. We will delete your online data and confirm by email, normally within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your chosen name, anonymous account, solo scores, and online duel results and win/loss record. Data saved only on your phone (coins, items, settings) is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Other rights

You can also email us to access, correct or export your data. See the privacy policy.
