Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Blokka is a puzzle game published by G-Games ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Contact: mortadamortada313m@gmail.com.

Summary

You can play Blokka without an account. If you choose to join the online features, we store a small amount of data (a name you choose, an anonymous account id, and your scores and duel results). We do not collect your email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or location. We do not sell your data and the game currently shows no ads.

Data stored only on your phone

Your progress (coins, unlocked items, best scores, settings, daily gift streak) and the name you choose are saved on your phone. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us unless you join online. It is removed when you uninstall the game.

Data we collect if you join online

When you tap JOIN, we create an anonymous account (no email, phone number or password). We then store: the name you choose; your anonymous account id; your finished solo scores; the results, scores and move lists of your online duels, and your win/loss counts; reports you send us about other players, and reports made about you. Your name, scores and duel wins are visible to other players on the leaderboards. Our hosting provider also processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the service.

Why we use your data and our legal basis

To run online features, match duels and show leaderboards (performance of the service you asked for, and your consent when you tap JOIN). To prevent cheating, abuse and offensive names, and to keep the service secure (our legitimate interests). To answer your requests and comply with the law. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your online data (see below).

Who we share data with

Online data is stored and processed for us by Supabase, our hosting provider, on servers in the European Union (Germany, Frankfurt region). Supabase acts as our processor and may use sub-processors, including outside the EU, under contractual safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for advertising. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep data

Online data is kept until you delete it or ask us to. Reports about names or behaviour are kept only as long as needed to handle them. Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the California CCPA/CPRA and similar laws) you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or export your data, object to our processing, and withdraw consent. The simplest way to delete is in the game: Settings, then Delete my online data, which removes your online profile, scores and duel record. You can also email us. If you are in the EU/UK you may complain to your local data protection authority. We do not discriminate against anyone for using these rights.

Do not sell or share

We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Advertising and purchases

This version of the game shows no ads and has no in-app purchases. Before we add ads or purchases, we will update this policy, name the provider, explain any advertising identifier use, and ask for your consent where the law requires it. Purchases, if added, are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store and we do not receive your payment card details.

Names and reports

Names are filtered for offensive words. Other players can report a name, and we may change or remove names and remove scores or accounts that break our rules.

Security

We use access rules on our database, anonymous accounts and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data. If a breach affects you we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

Blokka is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where your local law requires a higher age). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has joined online, contact us and we will delete the account.

International players

The game can be played worldwide. If you use online features from outside the EU, your data is processed in the EU as described above.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will tell you in the app about important changes.
