Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Blokka is a puzzle game made by G-Games. You can contact us at mortadamortada313m@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (coins, unlocked bundles and arenas, best scores, settings and your daily gift streak) and the name you choose are saved on your phone. None of this is sent anywhere unless you choose to join online.

If you join online

When you tap JOIN, we create an anonymous account for you (no email, phone number or password). We then store your chosen name, your anonymous account id, your finished solo scores, and the results and move lists of your online duels, including your win and loss counts. Your name and scores are visible to other players on the leaderboards. We do not collect your email, phone number, contacts or location.

Our service provider

Online data is stored with Supabase, our hosting provider. To run the service it may process technical data such as your IP address.

Advertising

This version of the game shows no ads. If ads are added, this policy will be updated before they appear.

How we use the data

To run the game, match duels, show leaderboards and prevent cheating. We do not sell your data.

Deleting your data

In Settings, tap Delete my online data to remove your online profile, scores and duel record. Data saved on your phone is removed when you uninstall the game. You can also ask us by email.

Children

Blokka is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where your local law requires). If you believe a child has joined online, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
